# UX design review: Depot run 1, stage 4

Reviewer: independent (did not write the spec). Repository read-only at `224f9e39` (the audit was taken at `cba4ebe`). Date: 2026-11-17.

Under review: `out/docs/ux/` (README.md, patterns.md UXS-01 to UXS-19, personas-and-journeys.md, floor-apps.md, usability-programme.md, rollout.md).
Checked against: `ux-audit.md` (UX-001 to UX-050), `docs/prd/part-00-thesis.md`, `docs/PRD.md` section 3, `apps/web/docs/*.md`, `apps/web/src`, `apps/{technician,warehouse-mobile,customer-portal,supplier-portal}`, `services/audit/api/openapi.yaml` and `services/audit/internal`, `architecture-delta.md`.

## Summary

The spec is strong. Every standard has a rule, a reason, real examples, acceptance criteria and a named enforcement. Shrinking allowlists stop the standards passing by default. Nearly every file, test, route and line number it cites is accurate (see "Correctness against the code"). The audit-service gaps are real. The usability gate is designed so that it cannot pass without a measurement.

I found no Blockers and 9 Majors. The Majors are:

- **Sequencing.** Four High findings, and the capture of reasons for sensitive actions, land late in the rollout.
- **Enforcement holes.** Two of the five banned-copy regexes miss the examples they are meant to catch, and the registry of audited actions is never checked for completeness.
- **Auditability that can pass without real data.** The History panel is only proven in the demo build, and the audit service accepts a "who" supplied by the caller.
- **Gaps for people.** Personas from PRD section 3 are missing; onboarding and the new-user guided flows are not covered; accessibility checking is weak (the automated scan comes last, there is no manual assistive-technology pass, and one shortcut conflicts with screen readers).
- **A gate that may stop releases.** The usability gate is likely to freeze releases before its baseline can be recruited.

## End-user friendliness and auditability: verdict

- **Floor, clerks and portals:** well served. The budgets are ambitious and mostly realistic, except the arithmetic in R-10. Offline handling, scanning rules, error copy, defaults, the line grid and bulk actions are thorough.
- **Gaps:**
  - personas from PRD section 3 (R-06)
  - onboarding and first-run journeys (R-07)
  - manual accessibility testing (R-08)
  - recovering from mistakes on the floor (R-15)
  - telling existing users what changed (R-19)
- **Auditability:** the design is right: who, when, what and why on every record; history that cannot be edited; an honest "not recorded" state; export with verification; cross-record search; a "Waiting on others" tab; masked values whose reveal is audited. But trusting that trail needs three things: a verified actor (R-04), real ingestion before M2 is declared (R-03), and reason capture enforced early and completely (R-05).
