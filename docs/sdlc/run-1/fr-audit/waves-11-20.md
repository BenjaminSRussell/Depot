core is solid.

## Top gaps (ranked by user/business impact)
1. FR-18.2 / FR-18.8: posting receipts and invoices does not touch inventory transactions (RECEIVED/PURCHASED). So procure-to-pay never updates on-hand stock, and AP is not linked to stock. Receiving needs the site, line ids and unit conversion that inventory requires (#96). Slice size: M.
2. FR-12.5 (with FR-12.6, FR-12.8): journal lines hold one currency only (no `amount_rpt`, no rate override, no multi-currency entries), and the FX revaluation math in fx is never posted. Foreign-currency AP, AR and period-end revaluation cannot be booked correctly. Slice size: L.
3. FR-19.9 / FR-19.8: a posted payment cannot be reversed or voided, and bank rejections (pain.002 RJCT, NACHA returns) are parsed but never applied, so items do not re-open. Treasury errors therefore need manual GL work. Slice size: M.
4. FR-19.7: NACHA, check (MICR) and positive-pay generators exist as pure code, but payment runs emit only pain.001. US payment methods cannot be used end to end. Slice size: M.
5. FR-15.9: there is no row-level dimension security. Anyone holding the reporting duty sees every cost centre in statements, drill-down and exports, which is a Must and a common audit finding. Slice size: L.
6. FR-14.5 (all of wave 14 UI): apps/web has no close module. Period status, the two-approver reopen, the checklist board, allocations, accruals and year-end are API-only, so controllers cannot run a close from the product. Slice size: M.
7. FR-19.22: there is no dual approval or out-of-band notice when a vendor bank account is created or changed. Payables only binds the account at proposal approval. This is the main AP fraud control. Slice size: S–M.
8. FR-19.10 / FR-19.16: prepayments and payment calendars (bank-holiday shifting) are both Must and both missing. Down payments to suppliers and correct value dates are basic AP needs. Slice size: M each.
9. FR-18.7 (with FR-18.5): invoice variances are computed but not routed anywhere, and the matching policy is fixed defaults with no vendor/item scoping. AP clerks have no exception queue. Slice size: M.
10. FR-13.9 / FR-13.11: tax is calculated, but the prepared tax journal lines are not returned for posting, and the tax settlement run posts nothing to the ledger. Tax liability accounting depends on callers. Slice size: M.

Also noted (not ranked): FR-17.16 policy framework not built (blocks the catalog/category policy rules); FR-18.11 charge allocation not built; several
built back-end features have no web screen (ERS, invoice adjustments, service entry sheets, plan/consignment settlements, AP aging, tax
certificates/withholding/settlement, ledger sets).
