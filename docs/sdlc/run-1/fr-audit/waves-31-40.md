## Top gaps (ranked by user/business impact)
1. FR-31.5 (with FR-31.4): there is no wave record, wave template, work template, HTTP route or screen for wave release, and fulfillment never creates warehouse work, so released sales orders cannot become pick work without a hand-built API call. This blocks document-driven warehousing. Slice size: L.
2. FR-31.8: loads, load building and load confirmation do not exist, so the warehouse cannot ship a load, post packing slips or emit `load.shipped`. The 856 and TMS hand-offs fall back to fulfillment for the same reason. Slice size: M.
3. FR-32.1: the handheld app has no login. Keycloak device flow and PIN re-auth are a handoff ("The app has no auth yet"), and the Android shell with native scanner intents (FR-32.10) is not built. This is a security and rollout blocker for every mobile flow. Slice size: M.
4. FR-33.3: association matching works, but no receiving, production or shipping service calls the quality-order API. Inspections are never triggered automatically, and holds (FR-33.5) depend on that trigger. Slice size: M (hooks in waves 18/21/28).
5. FR-34.8 (Must, Not built): no freight accrual is posted at ship confirmation and nothing is reversed on invoice match, so freight expense and accrued freight (2150) are wrong in the GL. The ledger posting client already exists in platform/ledgerclient. Slice size: S–M.
6. FR-39.7 (with FR-39.15): PPV and COGS-by-component postings are previews only. Nothing reaches the ledger from receipt or invoice events, so standard-cost inventory valuation and variance reporting are incomplete. FR-39.9 (the variance workspace) is also Not built. Slice size: M.
7. FR-40.2 (Must, Not built): the intercompany mirror saga and its documents (SO→PO, packing slip→receipt, invoice→vendor invoice) and IcDocumentLink do not exist. Intercompany trade is manual in both entities, and FR-40.3, FR-40.4 and FR-40.12 stay preview-only until it lands. Slice size: L.
8. FR-37.6: assets can only be acquired by hand at book creation. Acquisition from an AP invoice line, project CIP capitalization or an inventory issue is a handoff, so capex flows double-key into fixed assets. Slice size: M.
9. FR-36.21 (Must, Not built; FR-36.22 and FR-36.23 also Not built): there are no treasury payment requests, bank-to-bank transfers or payment routing, so cash movements between house banks happen outside the system. Slice size: M.
10. FR-31.16 (Must, Not built): there is no TRANSFER_ISSUE or TRANSFER_RECEIPT work and LP identity is not kept across sites. Inter-site transfers exist only as inventory documents (wave 16), with no warehouse execution. Slice size: L.
